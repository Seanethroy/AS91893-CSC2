Culture Behind the Dishes

🍲 Adobo

Adobo is widely regarded as the national dish of the Philippines. The use of vinegar to preserve meat is a traditional Filipino method.

🥟 Lumpia

Lumpia has Chinese origins but became a Filipino favorite, especially during celebrations and family gatherings.

🍧 Halo-Halo

"Halo-Halo" means mix-mix. This colorful dessert represents the Filipino joy of combining many flavors and textures.
