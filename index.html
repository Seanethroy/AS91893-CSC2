Culture Behind the Dishes

🍲 Adobo

Adobo is widely regarded as the national dish of the Philippines. The use of vinegar to preserve meat is a traditional Filipino method passed down through generations.

🥟 Lumpia

Lumpia has Chinese origins but has become a beloved Filipino party staple. It symbolizes abundance and is a must-have during celebrations and family gatherings.

🍧 Halo-Halo

"Halo-Halo" literally means "mix-mix". This iconic dessert showcases the Filipino creativity in combining many flavors and textures into one refreshing treat.

Classic Chicken Adobo

×

Ingredients (Serves 4-6)

  • 1.5 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 1 whole head garlic, crushed
  • ¾ cup soy sauce
  • ½ cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 4-5 dried bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine chicken, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, water, peppercorns, and bay leaves. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered for 35-40 minutes until tender.
  3. Reduce sauce until thickened.
  4. Optional: Fry the chicken pieces for extra flavor and color.
  5. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Lumpia Shanghai

×

Ingredients (Makes ~40 pieces)

  • 500g ground pork
  • 1 carrot, finely grated
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 40-50 lumpia wrappers
  • Oil for deep frying

Steps

  1. Mix all filling ingredients thoroughly.
  2. Place filling on wrapper and roll tightly. Seal with water.
  3. Deep fry in hot oil until golden brown and crispy.
  4. Drain on paper towels and serve with sweet chili sauce.

Halo-Halo

×

Ingredients (Per Serving)

  • 1 cup shaved ice
  • Sweetened red beans and white beans
  • Nata de coco and kaong
  • Ube halaya (purple yam jam)
  • Ube or mango ice cream
  • Sliced banana, jackfruit, and mango
  • Evaporated milk or condensed milk

Steps

  1. Layer beans and fruits at the bottom of a tall glass.
  2. Fill with shaved ice.
  3. Top with ube halaya, ice cream, and drizzle generously with milk.
  4. Mix everything together before eating. Enjoy!
