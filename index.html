Classic Chicken Adobo

×

Ingredients (Serves 4-6)

  • 1.5 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 1 whole head garlic, crushed
  • ¾ cup soy sauce
  • ½ cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 4-5 dried bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine chicken, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, water, peppercorns, and bay leaves. Marinate 30 mins or overnight.
  2. Bring to boil, then simmer covered for 30-40 minutes until tender.
  3. Reduce sauce until thickened.
  4. Optional: Fry chicken pieces for extra flavour.
  5. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Lumpia Shanghai

×

Ingredients (Makes ~40 pieces)

  • 500g ground pork
  • 1 carrot, finely grated
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 40-50 lumpia wrappers
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Mix all filling ingredients thoroughly.
  2. Wrap in lumpia wrappers and seal.
  3. Deep fry until golden and crispy.
  4. Serve with sweet chilli or vinegar sauce.

Halo-Halo

×

Ingredients (Per Serving)

  • 1 cup shaved ice
  • Sweetened red & white beans
  • Nata de coco & kaong
  • Ube halaya
  • Ube or mango ice cream
  • Fresh fruits (banana, jackfruit, mango)
  • Evaporated milk

Steps

  1. Layer beans and fruits at the bottom of a tall glass.
  2. Add shaved ice.
  3. Top with ube halaya, ice cream, and milk.
  4. Mix well before eating. Enjoy!
