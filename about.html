Our Story

Filipino food is more than just delicious meals, it is love served on a plate. It carries the warmth of family gatherings, the joy of fiestas, and the rich history of our beautiful 7,641 islands.

At Kusina Pilipinas, we celebrate the heart and soul of authentic Filipino cuisine. Our goal is to preserve traditional recipes and make them easy for both overseas Filipinos and international food lovers to recreate at home.

Our food is a beautiful fusion of Malay, Chinese, Spanish, and American influences, resulting in the uniquely Pinoy flavors we all cherish. From the iconic Chicken Adobo to the festive Halo-Halo, every dish tells a story of resilience, creativity, and bayanihan.

Why We Cook

Whether you're craving the taste of home, teaching your kids how to cook Filipino dishes, or simply exploring new cultures through food, we’re here to help you bring the warmth of Kusina Pilipinas to your table.

Food connects us. Let’s keep our culinary heritage alive, one recipe at a time.
